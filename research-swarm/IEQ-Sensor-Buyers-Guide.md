# IEQ Sensor Buyer's Guide for Senior Living

## Executive Summary

Selecting the right Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ) sensors for senior living facilities requires balancing accuracy, compliance, ease of use, and total cost of ownership. This guide compares five leading sensors across technical specifications, WELL v2 compliance levels, and senior living suitability.

**Key Finding:** For senior living facilities pursuing WELL v2 certification, the Kaiterra Sensedge Mini offers the best balance of compliance, accuracy, and long-term value. For budget-conscious facilities, the Awair Element provides excellent functionality at a lower price point.

## ROI Calculator

### Formula-Based ROI Calculator

Use these formulas to calculate your facility's return on investment:

#### Basic ROI Formula
```
ROI (%) = [(Annual Benefits - Annual Costs) / Initial Investment] × 100
```

#### Annual Benefits Components

| Benefit Category | Formula | Typical Range |
|-----------------|---------|---------------|
| **Reduced HVAC Energy** | (Sq Ft × $0.15-$0.45) | $0.15-$0.45/sq ft/year |
| **Reduced Respiratory Illness** | (Residents × 0.05 × $2,500) | $125/resident/year |
| **Reduced Staff Sick Days** | (Staff × 0.03 × $200/day) | $6/staff member/year |
| **Insurance Premium Reduction** | (Annual Premium × 0.02-0.05) | 2-5% of premium |
| **Marketing/Competitive Advantage** | (New Residents × $5,000) | Varies |
| **WELL Certification Value** | (Property Value × 0.03-0.07) | 3-7% value increase |

#### Annual Costs

```
Annual Costs = (Device Count × Annual Subscription) + 
               (Device Count × Annual Maintenance) + 
               (Device Count × Annual Calibration/Replacement)
```

## Deployment Checklist

### Phase 1: Planning & Assessment (Weeks 1-2)

#### ☐ Regulatory & Compliance Review
- [ ] Review state/local senior living IAQ requirements
- [ ] Determine if pursuing WELL v2 certification
- [ ] Identify LEED or other green building goals
- [ ] Review fire code requirements for sensor placement
- [ ] Check HIPAA implications if monitoring individual rooms

#### ☐ Facility Assessment
- [ ] Document facility layout and square footage
- [ ] Identify high-priority zones (dining, common areas, memory care)
- [ ] Map existing HVAC zones and controls
- [ ] Assess Wi-Fi coverage throughout facility
- [ ] Identify power outlet availability vs. need for hardwiring
- [ ] Document current IAQ complaints or issues

#### ☐ Stakeholder Alignment
- [ ] Present business case to executive leadership
- [ ] Involve facilities/maintenance team early
- [ ] Consult with nursing staff on resident needs
- [ ] Engage IT department on network requirements
- [ ] Review with legal/compliance on data privacy

### Phase 2: Procurement (Weeks 3-4)

#### ☐ Vendor Selection
- [ ] Request quotes from 3+ vendors
- [ ] Negotiate volume pricing (see Vendor Negotiation Tips below)
- [ ] Review warranty terms and support SLAs
- [ ] Confirm API access and data ownership terms
- [ ] Validate WELL v2 compliance documentation

#### ☐ Technical Requirements
- [ ] Confirm Wi-Fi network capacity (2.4GHz vs 5GHz)
- [ ] Verify firewall rules for cloud connectivity
- [ ] Plan VLAN segmentation if needed
- [ ] Document IP allocation if using Ethernet
- [ ] Confirm BACnet/Modbus compatibility if integrating with BMS

### Phase 3: Installation (Weeks 5-6)

#### ☐ Pre-Installation
- [ ] Create sensor placement map with device IDs
- [ ] Label all sensors with location codes
- [ ] Brief installation team on senior living sensitivities
- [ ] Schedule installation during low-activity hours
- [ ] Prepare resident/family communication

#### ☐ Installation Best Practices
- [ ] Mount at breathing height (3-6 feet) for occupied spaces
- [ ] Avoid direct sunlight, vents, and windows
- [ ] Maintain 3+ feet from doors and windows
- [ ] Ensure clear Wi-Fi signal at each location
- [ ] Document exact GPS coordinates for each sensor
- [ ] Photograph each installation for records

#### ☐ Specific Location Guidelines

| Location | Quantity | Height | Special Considerations |
|----------|----------|--------|----------------------|
| Dining Room | 1-2 | 4-5 ft | Away from kitchen exhaust |
| Common Lounge | 1-2 | 4-5 ft | Central location |
| Memory Care Unit | 1 per 1,000 sq ft | 4-5 ft | Tamper-resistant mounting |
| Admin Office | 1 | 4-5 ft | Near HVAC return |
| Sample Resident Rooms | 2-5 | 4-5 ft | Resident consent required |
| Activity Room | 1 | 4-5 ft | Away from craft supplies |
| Wellness/Clinic | 1 | 4-5 ft | Medical-grade accuracy preferred |

### Recommended Alert Thresholds for Senior Living

| Parameter | Warning Threshold | Critical Threshold | Rationale |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **PM2.5** | >25 µg/m³ | >50 µg/m³ | EPA standard; elderly more vulnerable |
| **CO2** | >800 ppm | >1,200 ppm | Cognitive impairment risk in elderly |
| **TVOCs** | >500 ppb | >1,000 ppb | Respiratory irritation threshold |
| **Temperature** | <68°F or >78°F | <65°F or >82°F | Elderly temperature sensitivity |
| **Humidity** | <30% or >60% | <20% or >70% | Respiratory and mold concerns |
| **Formaldehyde** | >20 ppb | >50 ppb | Cancer risk; elderly sensitivity |

## Vendor Negotiation Tips

### Pre-Negotiation Preparation

1. **Research Competitive Pricing**
   - Get quotes from all five vendors in this guide
   - Check GSA pricing if you're a non-profit or government-affiliated
   - Look for end-of-quarter or end-of-year timing for better deals

2. **Understand Your Leverage**
   - Volume: 10+ units typically qualifies for tiered pricing
   - Multi-facility: If you operate multiple properties, negotiate enterprise pricing
   - Reference potential: Offer to be a case study or reference
   - Long-term commitment: Multi-year agreements often yield 15-25% discounts

3. **Prepare Your Requirements Document**
   - Exact sensor count and locations
   - Required certifications (WELL, RESET, etc.)
   - Integration requirements (BACnet, API, etc.)
   - Support and warranty expectations
   - Data ownership and export requirements

### Negotiation Tactics

#### 1. Bundle for Better Pricing

#### 3. Timing Strategies

- **Quarter-End (March, June, September, December):** Vendors have sales quotas
- **End of Fiscal Year:** Budget flush periods yield better deals
- **New Product Launch:** Previous generation often discounted 20-40%
- **Trade Shows:** Show specials typically 10-15% below list

#### 4. Vendor-Specific Intelligence

**Kaiterra (Sensedge Mini)**

- Will often include first sensor module replacement free

- Ask for "WELL certification support package" (documentation help)

### Sample Negotiation Script

**Opening:**
> "We're evaluating IEQ sensors for our 100-bed senior living facility. We're comparing solutions from [Competitor A], [Competitor B], and your company. We need 10 units with full WELL v2 compliance documentation and enterprise dashboard access. What can you offer us?"

**Pushback on Price:**
> "Your quote is 20% higher than [Competitor]. We prefer your [specific feature], but budget is a constraint. What can you do to meet their pricing?"

**Value-Add Requests:**
> "If you can include 3-year warranties, free sensor module replacements, and on-site training, we can sign this week and serve as a reference customer."

**Closing:**
> "This looks good. Before I present to our board, can you confirm this includes [list negotiated items] in writing? We'd like to start with a 30-day pilot of 2 units."

### Contract Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Data Lock-in:** Ensure you can export historical data if switching vendors
❌ **Auto-Renewal:** Require 60-day notice for renewal, not automatic
❌ **Exclusive Clauses:** Avoid restrictions on using competing sensors
❌ **Vague Support SLAs:** Insist on specific response times in writing
❌ **Proprietary Calibration:** Ensure sensors can be calibrated by third parties
❌ **Cloud-Only Access:** Require offline/local data access capability

## Appendix: Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **IEQ** | Indoor Environmental Quality - encompasses air quality, thermal comfort, lighting, and acoustics |
| **WELL v2** | WELL Building Standard version 2 - certification for healthy buildings |
| **RESET** | International building standard for continuous air quality monitoring |
| **PM2.5** | Particulate matter smaller than 2.5 micrometers - fine dust |
| **PM10** | Particulate matter smaller than 10 micrometers - coarse dust |
| **TVOCs** | Total Volatile Organic Compounds - chemical off-gassing |
| **HCHO** | Formaldehyde - specific VOC of concern in buildings |
| **BACnet** | Building Automation and Control networks protocol |
| **Modbus** | Serial communication protocol for industrial equipment |
| **TCO** | Total Cost of Ownership - includes purchase, operating, and disposal costs |
